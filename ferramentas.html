Nota de campo

Comparison before commitment. Every prompt that matters gets run past a small arena of models first - the winner sets the direction, the losers reveal what the instruction was missing.

Fragmento de prompt

Same brief, three models. Rank for clarity, tone and usefulness. Tell me what the losers got wrong.

O que sai daí

A ranked decision with a reason attached - so the choice of model is a documented step, not a habit.
